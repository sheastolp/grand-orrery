# Maps

Rough sketches, not to scale.

- `region.png`: Verrowen around Stoutwell. It shows the Kingdom and its capital, the Monster Wood (the Blight, the goblin lands, troll territory and how far adventurers from the west have gotten), the Woodland Road, the croplands, Stoutwell, the Ashen Reach (its training grounds, to the south), the river, the irrigation and the mills. `region.svg` is its editable source.
- `stoutwell.png`: inside the walls. It shows the four gates, the main roads from each gate to the town square and the lanes into each district, the districts and the slanted divide (slums along the south and SE), the Gathering Hall, the dried well, the Wellhouse, Monstradius and the research building, Murrow's home, Doric's smithy, the lumber yard, the river mills and the Ashen Reach to the south. `stoutwell.svg` is its editable source.
