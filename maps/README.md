# Maps

Rough sketches, not to scale.

- `region.png`: Verrowen around Stoutwell. It shows the Kingdom and its capital, the Monster Wood (the Blight, the goblin lands, troll territory and how far adventurers from the west have gotten), the Woodland Road, the croplands, Stoutwell, the Ashen Reach (its training grounds, to the south), the river, the irrigation and the mills, and to the southeast the lake where the river ends, the fishing settlement on its far shore, the corner of the mountain wall that runs along the east and the south, and the fishers' hidden pass. `region.svg` is its editable source.
- `stoutwell.png`: inside the walls. It shows the four gates, the main roads from each gate to the town square and the lanes into each district, the districts and the slanted divide (slums along the south and SE), the Gathering Hall, the dried well, the Wellhouse, Monstradius and the research building, Murrow's home, Doric's smithy, the lumber yard, the river mills and the Ashen Reach to the south. `stoutwell.svg` is its editable source.
- `lake.png`: the lake, close up. It shows where the river ends, the near-shore landing and the footpath back to the mills and Stoutwell, the fishing boats, the fishing settlement (5 to 10 folk) on the far shore, the corner of the mountain wall, the hidden pass the fishers keep secret and the Ashen Reach to the west, with the mountains' southern arm beyond it. `lake.svg` is its editable source.
