## Enchanted items

Objects that hold someone's mana. Objects hold magic but don't make it, so every one of these runs dry and needs charging again. Enchanted items made in the Kingdom carry the mark of a registered artificer. Unmarked ones, like everything from Monstradius, aren't illegal, but they make people ask who made them.

### Charged stones

#### 🔥 Hearthstone (Your idea)

- **Does:** Keeps a stove warm through the night without new wood.
- **Made:** A fire user charges a smooth river stone in Murrow's shop. Murrow sets the charge so it releases slowly instead of all at once.
- **Limits:** It lasts one night per charge and still needs embers to work with. It warms the fire but doesn't replace it. Rare and pricey.
- **Rules:** Magic needs something to work with, and objects hold magic but don't make it.
- **Made by:** Murrow
- **Also called:** Hearthstones

### Hauling tools

#### 🪝 Featherhook (Your idea)

- **Does:** Cuts a log's weight about in half while it's being dragged.
- **Made:** Iron hooks forged by Doric, then imprinted by Murrow with a wind pattern.
- **Limits:** It draws on the latent wind in whoever holds it, so it works better for humans than halflings. Holding it for too long leaves you winded.
- **Rules:** Latent magic, attunement, and the personal well.
- **Made by:** Doric, Murrow
- **Also called:** Featherhooks

### Wards

#### 🔔 Wardbell (Your idea)

- **Does:** Rings by itself when something large comes out of the Monster Wood.
- **Made:** A detection user's sense is set into the bell's metal and pointed toward the wood.
- **Limits:** It needs a detection user to refresh it every few weeks. It can't tell goblins from trolls, only big from small.
- **Rules:** Objects run dry, and magic stays limited.
- **Made by:** Murrow
- **Also called:** Wardbells

### Lenses

#### 🔍 Truthglass (Your idea)

- **Does:** Shows whether a coin, seal or signed paper is real.
- **Made:** A rare piece of clear quartz, charged with detection magic.
- **Limits:** It only answers real or fake. It can't tell you who forged something or why.
- **Story:** It's dangerous for anyone skimming the guard tithe, or for a crooked tax representative.
- **Made by:** Murrow

### Artificers' secrets

#### 🧭 Rift compass (Your idea)

- **Note:** Murrow's secret project
- **Does:** Points toward places where the worlds are thin.
- **Made:** Unknown, even to Murrow. It may use a kind of magic nobody in the village has.
- **Limits:** About 80% complete, and still a secret.
- **Story:** It ties the village to Arandur, the rifts, and the wider universe.
- **Made by:** Murrow

#### 🪞 Seeker's Lodestone (Your idea)

- **Note:** Glumtom's secret
- **Does:** An arrow of light rises from it and points toward another artificer.
- **Made:** A perfectly round, flat disc of mirrorstone, shaped by magic and drawn by a blend of many mana types.
- **Limits:** It only finds artificers. Glumtom could find Murrow with it, but he isn't looking, and Murrow doesn't know it exists.
- **Made by:** Glumtom
- **Also called:** Lodestone

#### 🌫️ Lasting hiding charm (Your idea)

- **Note:** Murrow's research, not made yet
- **Does:** Would hold Murrow's spell that hides him from magical detection, so it no longer wears off.
- **Made:** Not yet. The *Nondetection*-style spell he casts himself only lasts so long, and he's working out how to put it into an item.
- **Limits:** Until it's finished, nothing hides him from the Seeker's Lodestone for long.
- **Made by:** Murrow

### Marks

#### 🔏 Artificer's mark (Your idea)

- **Does:** The mark a registered artificer puts on every enchanted item made in the Kingdom.
- **Rules:** Every artificer the Kingdom finds goes into the register of artificers and is put to work, the way Glumtom was.
- **Limits:** Unmarked items aren't illegal to own, but they make people ask who made them. Nothing from Monstradius carries one.
- **Story:** Murrow's goods are a quiet risk if they ever reach the capital.

### Regalia

#### 👑 Embercrown (Your idea)

- **Note:** From a lore-keeper chat, 2026-11-06
- **Does:** A delicate metal crown set with gems that glow like embers, worn by the Kingdom's royals at official events in the capital. Worn outside formal occasions, it stirs whispers of court gossip and intrigue around the wearer.
- **Made:** Needs rare materials to craft. The ember glow is a fire user's mana stored in the gems, so like every enchanted item it runs dry and has to be charged again.
- **Limits:** Royals only, and only for ceremonies. The edge of the realm never sees it, and most of the capital only ever sees the Crown's banner.
- **Story:** Raises how much influence and authority people see in the wearer. Who charges it is a question for the register of artificers. Open question: where is it kept when it isn't worn? The Crown's seat beside the Rolls House is the obvious guess.

- **Made:** One or two villagers with detection magic find dead trees near the forest edge, and a group drags them out with wind users lightening the load, under a Guard escort. Humans do about 70% of the chopping, halflings the rest.

## Records & papers

The Kingdom runs on paper: a name written down can be taxed, drafted, fined or found.

#### 📌 Bounty notice (Your idea)

#### 📓 The Wandering Clerk's ledger (Your idea)

## Peddlers' wares

What the capital's and the road's peddlers sell off blankets, crates and barrels at the open stall in the capital's market square, and wherever the Wandering Clerk's road stops: secondhand, cheap, and mostly what it looks like. Magic in Verrowen is weak and objects hold mana but don't make it, so whatever a ware is said to do, it does about as much as anything else on the blanket. The peddlers' patter is the real enchantment. GuildScribe's merchant stocks its stall from this file. Any decided entry with a **Sold:** line can turn up there at that price, and its bonus goes onto the buyer's character sheet. **Pitch:** is how the peddler hawks it.

### Relics

Rare finds the peddlers turn up now and then, in a bottomless sack or a barrel of turnips, and can never say where they came from. Some may have come out of the Monster Wood. Others may have come through a rift from another world, where they had other names and maybe real power. In Verrowen, objects hold mana but don't make it, and a charged item works only for whoever's mana is in it, so whatever a relic once did, it's asleep now. Nobody at the stall knows how to wake one. The peddlers have no idea what they've got, so they price on a hunch: dear for a curiosity, a steal if any of it is real.
