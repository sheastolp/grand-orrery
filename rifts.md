# Through the Rifts

The worlds on the other side of the rifts, and where to go to play in them or follow them: a campaign, its Discord, a game. The [Orrery Explorer](https://tavernworks.dev/orrery/) shows each world's links on its document and on its people's pages, and the lore-keeper shows them under any reply about that world.

**How to fill it in:** each world is a `##` heading, a few bullets, and a table of links. Put a full `https://` address in the URL column. A row with no URL is kept but doesn't show, so you can add the label now and the link later. To add a world, copy the Midkemia block.

- **What it is:** one line about the world.
- **Document:** the file in this repo about it, if there is one.
- **People:** roster names whose pages get its links, separated by commas.
- **Also called:** other names the lore-keeper should notice, separated by commas.
