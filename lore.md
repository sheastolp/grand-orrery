# Verrowen lore

This file is the single source of truth the narrator reads. Edit it as the universe grows.

UNIVERSE: The Grand Orrery. It's the artificers' name for the universe, picturing its worlds as moving parts in one great mechanism. Only high-ranking and learned people know the name. People sometimes cross between worlds.
WORLD: Verrowen. This is a scholarly name found in books. Villages own few books, and only kingdoms have libraries, so common folk just say "the world."
TONE: Medieval fantasy with D&D-style monsters.
MAGIC: Common but weak. It assists work, it doesn't replace it. Types: elemental (wind, fire, earth), strength enhancement, detection. A fire user who can boil a pot of water counts as super skilled. Everyone has a well of mana. It refills more slowly than the body heals, and there's no visible bar. Running out (mana deprivation) causes sickness, dizziness or passing out. Elves are most magical, then humans, then halflings. People can have latent magic they don't know about. Objects hold mana but can't make it. A charged item normally works only for the person whose mana is in it.
ARTIFICERS: Extremely rare. They understand all magic, store other people's mana in objects, never lend their own strength, and can sense latent magic but not awaken it. A secret artificer item, the Seeker's Lodestone, can locate other artificers.
STOUTWELL: A 600-year-old walled village of about 220 people: about 72% human, 28% halfling, plus the Wellhouse family, its only elves, who keep to their tavern and are ostracized. It's known for its stout ale and ornate smithing. Its central well dried up 550 years ago because of the blight; the artificer Murrow recently built irrigation from the river. Gates face north, east, south and west. The west gate opens onto the croplands, the Woodland Road and the Monster Wood. Districts: NW is middle- to upper-class housing; NE is Murrow's shop Monstradius and his research building; SW is the shopping district with the Wellhouse tavern; the slums lie along the south and SE, with their own poorer housing and Doric's smithy. Near the center are the dried well and the Gathering Hall, where the Elderhold meets. Grain and lumber mills sit on the river outside the east wall; the lumber yard is inside by the east gate.
LIFE: Children are schooled together, then the Elders place them into groups (Village Workers, Herders, Farmers, Gatherers, the Guard, the Hearthcircle). People may switch jobs within reason. Homes burn firewood. Gatherers find dead trees with detection magic and drag them out with wind magic, under Guard escort.
DEFENSE AND LAW: The Guard is stout halfling shield-bearers plus human archers on the walls. Villagers rotate a couple of days a month. The halfling Guard is small and overworked. Punishments range from stocks and flogging to exile and execution. Weregild (payment in place of punishment) is accepted.
LEADERSHIP: The Hearthcircle is the ruling group, placed by talent, never by birth. The Elderhold is five halfling Elders who vote (Wendeline Brackenford, Fenwick Larkspur, Bertram Quillby, Ysolde Ambergate, Clementine Rook) plus Osric, their non-voting spokesperson and Leader. Bertram and Ysolde are secretly corrupt. Osric suspects but can't prove it, and he hides a surname tied to Kingdom nobility.
PEOPLE: Tobin Ashby (halfling, 33, blacksmith's apprentice; determined, loyal, efficient). Doric (halfling, 67, strict, proud master smith). Murrow (artificer over 550 years old, race unknown, hidden under a deep hood; cold and businesslike, never mean; fears the Kingdom). Hesketh Reeve (human, 48, Guard Captain; harsh but fair).
BEYOND: The Monster Wood (goblins, trolls, large beasts) lies to the west. An old blight is spreading through its deep woods, driving desperate goblins to raid Stoutwell's crops. Past the wood is a corrupt Kingdom and its capital, where the gnome artificer Glumtom works for the Kingdom, unwillingly. Merchants with escorts and a tax representative travel the Woodland Road. A secretive cult seeks a way to dream any weapon or monster into existence.
